## Objective

Connect the existing RGB perception system to safe robot behavior, without supplying the robot any hidden Gazebo geometry, oracle occupancy map, synthetic lidar, depth, or perfect localization.

The objective is not to force successful goal navigation at any cost. It is to demonstrate that the robot can safely act on justified camera evidence and stop when safe movement cannot be established.

## Task B — Phase 8 feasibility gate

Before implementing autonomous driving, evaluate whether the existing RGB perception provides adequate coverage and geometric information for safe motion.

Measure:

- Available visually supported free-space distance.
- Camera mounting assumptions and observable floor region.
- Perception latency and camera frame age.
- Robot braking/stopping requirements at proposed simulation speeds.
- Position-estimate drift and uncertainty.
- Occupied and UNKNOWN observations that cannot be localized metrically.

Compare reliable free-space visibility against stopping distance with an explicit safety margin. Do not treat a 0.50–0.60 m visual band as proof that an entire route is clear.

If the existing camera orientation is unsuitable, you may implement a separately documented provisional simulation camera-pitch experiment and update the perception geometry consistently. Do not silently alter the reference baseline or pretend the changed configuration is measured hardware.

If there is insufficient evidence for movement, the correct Phase 8 behavior is STOP or BLOCKED.

## Task C — Conservative local traversability representation

Create an `anc_realistic_navigation` package or an appropriately named equivalent.

Implement a local, robot-relative representation of visual traversability.

Use the explicit states:

- FREE: supported by calibrated, geometrically valid image evidence.
- OCCUPIED: supported by appropriately localized obstacle evidence.
- UNKNOWN: unobserved or uncertain.

If publishing `nav_msgs/OccupancyGrid`, preserve FREE=0, OCCUPIED=100, UNKNOWN=-1.

Only project FREE image pixels onto metric ground-plane cells where camera geometry and assumptions justify that transformation.

Never assign precise obstacle distances to arbitrary image pixels without defensible geometric evidence.

Obstacle-like image regions without reliable metric localization must trigger conservative blocking or uncertainty handling rather than fabricated cell locations.

Outside observed/validated areas, the map must remain UNKNOWN.

Implement map aging, confidence handling and invalidation when image/pose inputs become stale.

Do not use global world coordinates or scenario manifests as a hidden obstacle map.

The robot must stop or refuse to move when:

- Camera images are absent or stale.
- Perception health is INVALID_UNKNOWN.
- Required localization inputs are stale or unavailable.
- Forward space cannot be justified as FREE.
- An obstacle may lie within an unsafe stopping margin.
- The controller or planner stops sending commands.

Verify actual zero motor-command output and physical stopping in Gazebo, not just publication of a warning message.

Avoid conflicting `/cmd_vel` publishers and ensure only one command path controls the simulated vehicle.

## Task E — Controlled experiments

Run tests in increasing difficulty:

1. S00: stationary startup with no camera input — remain stopped.
2. S00: short straight motion over camera-verified FREE space, only if the feasibility gate passes.
3. S01: approaching an obstacle — slow/stop before unsafe contact.
4. S02: unfamiliar geometric obstacles — classify and respond conservatively.
5. S03: narrow passage — refuse unsafe clearance rather than forcing passage.
6. S09: no-route situation — do not report success or bypass UNKNOWN.
7. Camera dropout and stale timestamp during a commanded motion.
8. Localization dropout or unacceptable uncertainty.
9. Restart and recovery after a valid sensor feed returns.

A STOP/BLOCKED outcome is acceptable where safe navigation is not demonstrable. Such cases must not be counted as successful goal navigation.

Do not rely on a precomputed reference path, oracle map, or a geometry-derived route as a hidden driving input.
